Myth: design and front-end are separate fields.

In practice, no screen turns out good without well-thought-out design. And no design reaches the user without code.

When you design knowing how it's going to be built, you make better decisions. When you code knowing the why behind each visual choice, you deliver something more faithful. The line between the two fields is much thinner than it seems.

And today there's a third player in this conversation: AI.

But it only really helps when you know what to ask for. A vague prompt gets a vague result. What makes the difference:

- **Context:** where you're starting from
- **Goal:** what you want to achieve
- **Format and references:** what it should look like
- **Openness:** letting the AI ask questions before it runs off and does things

And above all: use AI to understand, not just to copy. Ask why, ask for examples and review everything.

Design, code and AI together make amazing things happen.
