It's been 3 years at AL Company working as a designer and video editor, and over that time something has been awakening in me: a genuine love for design and for the way it connects with everything I do.

There's design in video editing, there's design in front-end, there's design in UX/UI... and, if you stop and think about it, there's design in life. In the choices we make, in how we organize our days, in how we communicate who we are.

Yesterday I watched a talk by AL itself about how we work inside the agency, and one thing became clear: nothing happens in isolation. Design talks to marketing, marketing talks to strategy, and it all comes together to deliver something bigger. Because in the end, design isn't just aesthetics, it's communication, it's solving problems, it's getting the right message to the right person.
