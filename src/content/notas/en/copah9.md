Spreadsheets, a WhatsApp group and paper on a clipboard. That's how CopaH9 was going to be run.

My friend is a futsal coach, and together we've organized a few tournaments. That meant we needed to manage the athletes' games, and there was no tool for it at all. We'd run a few previous editions with Excel spreadsheets and even by hand, but it wasn't practical. So I came up with the idea of building a system to manage these tournaments.

At the 3rd edition of CopaH9, the system handled:

- 15 teams
- 4 age groups (U-4/5, 6/7, 8/9 and 10/11)
- 24 games, all in a single day
- 31 goals recorded

A match schedule, standings that update on their own, knockout brackets, a live scoreboard and a link (or QR code) so parents could follow everything from their phones, in real time, without installing anything.

What started as a futsal manager turned into a multi-sport tournament manager, and today it also handles volleyball, handball and basketball.

But what made me happiest wasn't the end result, it was how easy it is to run from anywhere, on any device. You can referee a game, enter the score and schedule the next round right from your phone, courtside.

The best way to validate a product is still this: a real problem, a real deadline and an event that's actually happening.
