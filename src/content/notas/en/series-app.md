Remember TV Time? It was the app I'd used for years to keep track of my shows, and it came to an end.

I went looking for a replacement and nothing won me over: either it was missing features, or the performance was way below what I was used to. That's when the thought hit me: "why not build one myself?"

And that's how series app was born.

Built from scratch, for the web, but fully responsive so you can use it on your phone. It's an app I made for myself, exactly the way I always wanted it, and that's what makes the project so special. My main inspirations were TV Time itself and Letterboxd, which helped me get this idea off the ground.

The main features:

- Show search with a complete catalog
- Marking episodes as watched, including "mark up to here", for anyone who jumped into a show partway through
- Show status: Watching, Want to Watch, Completed, Dropped
- Stats and history of everything you've ever watched
- And of course: I imported my entire TV Time history so I wouldn't lose anything

Under the hood, I used Next.js with TypeScript for the front-end, Supabase (Postgres) for the database and Tailwind for styling. And a special shout-out to TVmaze: a free, open TV show data API that powers the app's entire catalog: shows, seasons, episodes and premiere dates. Without it, none of this would exist.

And the project doesn't stop there: I still have a huge list of improvements I want to make. The app is alive, and every week it gets a little something new.

And there's one thing worth mentioning: I had a lot of help from AI on this journey, in the best possible way. I used AI like a teacher by my side: it explained concepts to me, guided me step by step and reviewed what I wrote. Instead of just handing me ready-made code, it made me really learn front-end, back-end and databases. It's proof that AI doesn't replace people who want to learn, it speeds them up.

You can try it right in your browser: [series-app-topaz.vercel.app](https://series-app-topaz.vercel.app/), or read the [case study](/en/projects/series-app-catalogo).
